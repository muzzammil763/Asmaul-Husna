```
Asmaul Husna brings you the 99 Beautiful Names of Allah in a calm, simple app that you can open anytime, anywhere. Each Name is shown in clear Arabic calligraphy, with its transliteration, English meaning, Urdu meaning and a short reflection.

"To Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them." (Al-A'raf 7:180)

ALL 99 NAMES IN ONE PLACE
• Every Name in the order narrated by at-Tirmidhi
• Beautiful Arabic calligraphy with full harakat
• Transliteration, so you can say each Name correctly
• English and Urdu meanings side by side

LEARN AND REFLECT
• A dedicated page for every Name
• A short reflection on what each Name means for us
• Move to the next or previous Name with one tap
• Copy any Name, with its meanings and reflection, to share with family and friends

FIND ANY NAME QUICKLY
• Search by transliteration, meaning or number
• Browse as a detailed list or a clean grid

FAVOURITES
• Tap the heart to save the Names close to your heart
• See all your favourites in one tap

MADE FOR YOU
• Light and dark themes, or follow your phone's setting
• Choose what shows on each card: transliteration, English meaning, Urdu meaning
• Make the Arabic text larger or smaller to suit your eyes

PRIVATE AND FULLY OFFLINE
• Works without internet
• No account and no sign-up
• No ads and no tracking
• Your favourites and settings stay on your phone

Whether you are memorising the 99 Names, teaching them to your children or simply want to remember Allah through His Names during the day, Asmaul Husna keeps them with you, beautifully and quietly.
```
